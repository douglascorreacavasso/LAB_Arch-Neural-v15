## 🎯 Philosophy

The brain is **ONE thing**. There's no "code outside the brain" and "JSON inside the brain" — it's a single structure with specialized regions. Just like the human brain: motor cortex is both anatomy AND physiology, not separated in files.

That's why V15.1 FINAL delivers **`arch_neural_v15_final.js`** — **a single 4MB file** with everything inside:

- ✅ Graph structure (anatomy)
- ✅ Execution engine (physiology)
- ✅ Social reflexes
- ✅ Turing cortex (variables, loops, conditionals)
- ✅ Cognitive cortex (hypotheses, simulation, analogy, reverse-engineering, metacognition)
- ✅ Statistical cortex (10 REAL math motors)
- ✅ Self-modification (learner, escape valve, biological evolution)
- ✅ **Serialized brain embedded** (115 sub-networks, 2400+ nodes)

**14 regions stacked in chronological project order, in a single file.**

No external APIs. No LLM. No internet required. Runs in browser, pure JavaScript, **fully offline and deterministic**.
